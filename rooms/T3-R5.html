Latency and a toy budget

  • Five minutes
  • Visual
  • Worked example
  • Quiz
  • Ecosystem
  • Sources and go deeper

Learn in five minutes

  • Latency is time, usually milliseconds.
  • An average hides the slow tail. Lab 08 looks at a stage p95.
  • A budget is a limit you chose for a lesson, not a vendor SLA.
  • Voice feels worse than chat at the same delay because the person is waiting in real time.
  • Confirm any published SLA before you repeat it. This page states none.

If the speech stage is 900 toy milliseconds and the model stage is 200, the speech stage is the one to study. The average of the two would hide that.
