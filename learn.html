Finish in 10 minutes

Read only these opening rooms, export one diagram, and stop.

  1. How this learning lab works — This site is a free learning lab about AI omni-channel agents.
  2. Tokens, not words — A model reads tokens, which may be parts of words.
  3. A job, a shape, and a test — A wish is not a job. Name the reader and the done check.
  4. Why retrieve — A model does not contain your private notes.
  5. An agent is a loop — An agent here means the model can choose a tool, see the result, and continue.
  6. The problem MCP names — Tools written for one app are hard to reuse in another.
  7. Omni-channel, for a learner — Omni-channel here means one assistant pattern that can meet a person on more than one channel.
  8. One GPU, then a cluster — A GPU is a processor often used for the matrix math in model training and serving.
  9. Speech in, a model, speech out — LiveKit's pipeline page names a cascaded pipeline: speech-to-text, then a language model, then text-to-speech.
  10. Transcription of a recording — Wikipedia defines speech analytics as analyzing recorded calls to gather customer information.
  11. Handoff to a person — A handoff in this lab means a person continues the turn.
  12. Words for this ecosystem — Wikipedia defines speech analytics as analysis of recorded calls.
