  2. Wrong fact: was the span in the pack? If not, refuse or fix the index.
  3. Tool error: the model is quoting your handler.
  4. Should have refused: the gate failed, not the tone.
  5. Slow phone turn: time ASR, model, and TTS separately.
  6. Change one thing. Rescore the same tasks.
